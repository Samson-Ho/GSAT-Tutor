# 國綜 — Trends (考試說明例題 → 參考試卷 → 110試辦 → 111–115學測)

GSAT-Tutor's analysis of how the 考試說明 has actually been applied. These are observed patterns, not official policy: never tell a student a topic "will" appear.

## 1. Structure: settled since 113

| | 單選 | 多選 | 混合題 | 非選 total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 110試辦, 111, 112 | 25 × 2 = 50 | 7 × 4 = 28 | 2 題組 = 22 (第一題14 + 第二題8) | 18 |
| **113, 114, 115** | **24 × 2 = 48** | **7 × 4 = 28** | **1 題組 = 24** (32–36) | **20** |

Takeaways:
- Default mock paper: **24 單選 + 7 多選 + one 5-item 混合題組 (32–36: 3 非選 + 2 單選, or 4 非選 + 1 單選)**, 90 分鐘.
- The 113–115 混合題組 is a **multi-text set** (甲乙丙丁) with a framing essay that supplies a concept, then applies it to literary texts:
  - 113: 回憶書寫 (甲 theory of 自主／不由自主的回憶; 乙 Ackerman on smell and memory) applied to 琦君〈髻〉, 李煜〈浪淘沙〉, 辛棄疾〈破陣子〉.
  - 114: 「概念框架」 explained through a chat dialogue, applied to 〈虬髯客傳〉 and 《西遊記》.
  - 115: 「聽見她的聲音」: 歸有光's women relatives (〈項脊軒志〉〈先妣事略〉〈祭外姑文〉), 李欣倫〈水面下〉, and an oral-history record (《消失中的台灣阿嬤》).
- 非選 sub-items are 2分 or 4分, with 10–40字 limits. Single-choice items inside the set test judgments (①② 研判, 最適當的解讀).

## 2. Item types in 第壹部分 (111–115)

**Openers (near-fixed positions):**
| Pos. | Type | 111 | 112 | 113 | 114 | 115 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 字音「讀音前後相同」 | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| 2 | 字形「完全沒有錯別字」 | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| 3 | 詞語／成語 or 填詞 | 成語 | 成語 | 文意 | 填詞 (聶華苓) | 資料＋應用文 |
| 4–5 | short text: 文意／填句／**古文排序** | 斯卡羅資訊框、王夢鷗 | 表格 (唐詩人問候詩)、說話角色 | 填句 (抱朴子)、**古文排序** | 丘成桐、填句 (王安石〈材論〉) | 右框解讀篇章、**古文排序 (5句)** |

**Multi-select (25/26–31/32) recurring types:**
- 字義「意義前後相同」 (every year: 111-26, 112-26, 113-27, 114-25, 115-25).
- 成語／詞語「運用適當」 (multi-select 113-25, 114-26, 115-26; single-choice 111-3, 112-3).
- 語法 by example: 「稍微」修飾動詞程度 (112-27); 「以」表目的 (113-26); 「則」表假設 (114-29). The stem defines the pattern with an example; no grammatical terms are required.
- 閱讀 multi-select on a modern essay, information texts or 文學史 (祕密, 賴和 poems, 臺灣海洋文化, 硃砂, 博物館, 桃花源, 文學的聲音, 樂曲).
- 題組 of 2 multi-selects on paired texts (屈原 112-31/32; 鄭成功 113-30/31; 晚明遊記 114-30/31; 表 115-30/31).

**題組 (2–5 items) dominate 單選 6–24.** Each year has 7–8 single-choice 題組.

**文言 share**: roughly 40% of items involve 文言 material (an informal GSAT-Tutor count, consistent with the 35–45% guideline). Classical texts come from 先秦諸子, 史傳, 筆記小說, 明清小品 and 清代臺灣文獻.

## 4. 混合題 non-choice patterns (rubrics)

Every 111–115 rubric uses only these tiers:

| Item value | Tiers |
|---|---|
| 2分 short extraction | 「能正確寫出參考答案之文句」2分／「未作答或答案完全錯誤」0分 |
| 4分 explanation | 「能寫出參考答案大意，且敘述完整」4分／「答案敘述部分錯誤或不完整」2分／「未作答或答案完全錯誤」0分 |
| 2分 with two components | 2分 both／1分 「僅正確寫出其中一項」／0 (113-33(2) 大腦: 杏仁核＋海馬迴) |

Preamble since 111: 「考生作答內容若在關鍵字詞、專有名詞上出現錯別字，或錯別字影響答案的完整性與正確性，都將酌予扣分。」 The 110試辦 was more lenient: 「偶有錯別字，但不影響文意判讀，錯別字不扣分」.

The 參考試卷 rubrics show richer tiers that remain legitimate models: 6分 = 6／4／2／0 (卷一-31 李清照); 勾選 1分 + 理由 4分 (卷一-34); 分項 2分 each with a 特殊評分原則 against splitting one point into two (卷二-33).

Typical 非選 tasks:
- **擷取** (2分): a phrase or term from the text (113-32(1) 自主的回憶; 113-33(2) 嗅覺; 112-37(1) 反映人性現實).
- **說明理由／原因** (4分): why X, from the text (114-34(1); 115-33(2); 113-32(2)).
- **概念應用** (4分): apply the framing concept to a literary text (113-36(2) 提醒物＝劍; 114-35 身分框架; 115-34 考量＋行動).
- **比較** (4分): two texts or two metaphors (113-33(1) 斷片 vs 照片褪色).
- **表格填寫** (參考 29, 33; 114-36(2) 示例＋作答).

## 5. Mock-paper blueprint (default, 113–115 model)

| Section | Items | Content plan |
|---|---|---|
| 單選 1 | 字音 | 4 pairs of 形聲 or textbook characters (A1) |
| 單選 2 | 字形 | 4 sentences, one fully correct (A1) |
| 單選 3–5 | short single items | 填詞／成語 (A2), 文意 or 填句 (B2), 古文排序 (A4/B2) |
| 單選 6–24 | 6–7 題組 of 2–3 items | Mix: 1 modern literary prose, 1–2 cross-field informational texts (one with a table or chart), 1 classical 史傳 or 筆記, 1 classical-plus-modern commentary pair, 1 poetry or 文學史 set, 1 Taiwan-related text |
| 多選 25–31 | 7 items | 字義 (A1), 成語運用 (A2), 語法 by example (A3), 2 reading multi-selects, 1 paired-text 題組 of 2 |
| 混合 32–36 | 1 題組, 24分 | Framing concept (甲) + 2–3 literary or real texts; 3 非選 (2+4 sub-parts) + 1–2 單選 (①② 研判) |

Keep 文言 at about 35–45% of items. Keep 教材選文 items within the A／B／C rule; prefer widely taught texts (A類).

## 6. Study priorities (for diagnosis and plans)

1. **Fixed-point basics** (items 1–2 and the 字義／成語 multi-selects, about 16–20分/year): spaced practice on 形聲字音, common 錯別字, 一字多義 in textbook sentences, misused idioms.
2. **①② 研判 discipline**: decide 符合／不符合／**無法判斷** strictly from the text. 「無法判斷」 is a frequent answer.
3. **Multi-text integration**: tracking which text says what (甲乙丙), and applying a concept from one text to another (the 混合題 core).
4. **Informational and non-continuous texts**: tables, 右框, and diagrams in cross-field topics.
5. **混合題 writing**: answer in the text's key words, cover every component the question asks for (理由＋行動, 前提＋推論), and stay within the 字數 limit. Precision beats elaboration.
6. **文學史 and 文化 knowledge** tied to 教材選文 (genres, authors, 應用文 conventions).
