# 數A — Trends (參考試卷 → 110試辦 → 111–115學測)

GSAT-Tutor's analysis of how the 考試說明 scope has actually been tested. Use it to weight topics, choose item styles, build mock papers and prioritize study plans. These are observed patterns, not official policy: never tell a student a topic "will" appear.

## 1. Paper structure: settled since 110試辦

| | 參考試卷 | 110試辦 | 111 | 112 | 113 | 114 | 115 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 單選 | 1–7 (35) | 1–6 (30) | 1–6 | 1–6 | 1–6 | 1–6 | 1–6 |
| 多選 | 8–13 (30) | 7–12 (30) | 7–12 | 7–12 | 7–12 | 7–12 | 7–12 |
| 選填 | 14–17 (20) | 13–17 (25) | 13–17 | 13–17 | 13–17 | 13–17 | 13–17 |
| 混合題 | 18 多選5 + 19 非選10 | 18 單3 + 19 非6 + 20 非6 | 3+6+6 | 3+6+6 | 3+4+8 | 3+6+6 | 3+4+8 |

Takeaways:
- Default to **6 單選 / 6 多選 / 5 選填 / 1 混合題組 (18 單選3分 + 19、20 非選)**, 100 分鐘, no calculator, formula sheet provided.
- The 混合題 split is either 6+6 or 4+8. In 4+8 papers, 19 is a short 說明/證明 or setup step and 20 is the substantial multi-part computation.
- 18 is always a quick 單選 that hands the student a key quantity or relation needed later (a "scaffold").

Counts are approximate because many items span two units, e.g. 113-11 方程組＋條件機率.

**Near-certain every year** (appeared in all five of 111–115):
- 計數 D-10-3 (1–2 items), 期望值 D-10-4 (1), 條件機率／獨立／貝氏 D-11A-2/3 (1), and **數據分析 D-10-2 (1), always with a twist** (see §3).
- 指數／對數 (N-10-3/4, A-11A-4, F-11A-4): 1–2 items, often as a modelling or calculator context.
- 空間向量／平面／直線 (G-11A-7~10): 2–3 items or the whole 混合題.
- 平面向量 (G-11A-1/6): 1 item, usually 線性組合 with areas or angle bisectors.
- 正餘弦定理 (G-10-7): 1–2 items, often combined with 倍角 (G-11A-5).
- 矩陣／線性變換／方程組 (A-11A-1~3, F-11A-3): 1–2 items, or the 混合題 (114).

**Very frequent** (4 of 5 years): 三次函數圖形特徵 F-10-2 (對稱中心, 近似直線), 三角函數圖形／疊合 F-11A-1/2, 數列／遞迴 N-10-6, 直線與圓 G-10-2/3/4.

**Rare or embedded only**: 絕對值不等式 (111-7 only; 參考／試辦 Q1–2), 空間概念 S-11A-1 (111-11), 極坐標 G-10-5 (111 混合題, 112-19), 集合／邏輯 (★＃, appear only as option wording).

## 3. Signature item patterns (model new items on these)

| Pattern | What it looks like | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 數據分析 with a twist | Transform data (unit change, linear map, log), then ask what happens to 平均／標準差／相關係數／迴歸直線; or compare two groups' regression lines; or project 2D data onto a line | 111-3 投影變異數, 112-3 (s, log t) 迴歸, 113-9 兩物種迴歸, 114-12 換單位與變數, 115-9 T 分數 |
| 加權平均 trap | Rates or averages of subgroups can't be averaged directly | 參考-10 失業率, 113-13 手機利潤 |
| 三次函數對稱中心 | $f(x)=a(x-h)^3+p(x-h)+k$; symmetric centre, 近似直線 near x=h, translations, f + g or quotient forms | 參考-9, 111-10, 113-3, 114-13, 115-12 |
| 疊合 / trig graph counting | Rewrite as $r\sin(x+\phi)$; count intersections or solutions on an interval; symmetry axes; sums of roots | 參考-12, 110-6, 112-12, 113-4, 114-5, 114-10, 115-11 |
| 機率 in real context | Rapid tests, lotteries, games, gacha, independence from a table, Bayes with "已知…的條件下" | 111-5, 111-13, 113-15, 114-1, 114-15, 115-1, 115-13 |
| 計數 with structure | Arrangements with blocks and order constraints, choosing with repetition, merging duplicates by category | 111-1, 112-4, 113-5, 114-3, 115-4 |
| 矩陣 as transformation | Rotation, reflection and shear matrices; composition; invariance (area scales by the absolute value of the 行列式 $\begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{vmatrix}$); fixed points; inverse existence | 112-11, 113-12, 114-18~20, 參考-6, 110-18~20 |

## 4. 混合題 (18–20) patterns

| Year | Theme | 18 (單選3) | 19 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 110試辦 | 線性變換 $\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}$ | a,b relation | find a,b (6) | 說明 P′Q′/PQ 為定值 (6) |
| 111 | 環狀區域掃描棒 | B 坐標 | 作圖＋cos＋極坐標 (6) | 扇形分割求面積 (6) |
| 112 | 坐標三角形與向量 | OP=2cosθ | Q 坐標＋說明 BQ=2AP (6) | 點到直線距離＋梯形面積 (6) |
| 113 | 空間：平面與夾角 | cos 夾角 | 證明不等式 (4) | c 的範圍＋OP 最小值 (8) |
| 114 | 旋轉矩陣 | c 值 | Q 坐標＋夾角 (6) | ∠OSP＋S 坐標 (6) |
| 115 | 平行六面體與外積 | 平行四邊形面積 | 平面方程式 (4) | 體積＋最遠距離 (8) |

Patterns:
- Almost always **geometry / vectors / space / matrices**, never pure statistics or counting.
- At least one 非選 part asks to **說明／證明** a relation (110-20, 112-19, 113-19), not just compute.
- Later parts reuse earlier results (承19題); a careful solution states which earlier result it uses.
- Answers are exact forms: radicals, π, fractions, polar coordinates [r, θ].

## 5. 情境 (素養) items

About 4–6 items per paper carry a real-world or academic context, mostly in 單選 1–6 and 選填 13–15. Contexts come from daily life and school (ice cream, calculators, salaries, drinks, phones, coins, temple lotteries, online games, concerts), science (drug decay, alloy wavelengths, species body measures, rapid tests), and 學測 culture (T 分數). The math stays standard once translated. Write context items the same way: the stem gives everything needed, no outside knowledge, and the context determines the model (which formula or quantity to use).

## 6. Difficulty profile (GSAT-Tutor estimate)

- 單選 1–2 are usually gentle; 3–6 rise to medium. 多選 options mix easy and hard; at least one option usually needs real reasoning (a counterexample, a boundary case).
- 選填 13–14 are medium; **15–17 are the hardest in Part 1** (multi-step geometry or space, optimization, new definitions).
- 混合題 20 is the hardest written item. Typical traps: endpoint or domain checks (113-20 頂點不在範圍內), rejecting extraneous cases (參考-16 鈍角不合), and justifying claims (說明題).
- Common traps students fall into: averaging rates, ignoring the domain of log, losing a case in counting, forgetting the 2D vs 3D distinction, sign errors in 外積, and assuming a regression line is symmetric (y on x vs x on y).

## 7. What changed from 參考試卷／試辦 to 111–115

- 單選 dropped from 7 to 6; 選填 grew from 4 to 5. The 混合題 settled on 單選 + 2 非選 rather than 多選 + 1 non-選.
- The 參考試卷 混合題 used an economics context (成本／獲利 polynomials). Real papers moved to geometric or matrix 混合題 with 說明 components.
- Rubrics became more explicit (114/115 list 滿分／部分給分／零分 tiers); see `grading.md`.
