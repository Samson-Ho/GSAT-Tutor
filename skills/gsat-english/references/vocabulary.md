# 高中英文參考詞彙表 — Complete Word List (111學年度起適用)

Every entry of the official 大考中心《高中英文參考詞彙表》(109年7月公告, 111學年度起適用), transcribed from `assets/papers/vocabulary-list-111.pdf` (依級別排序 p13–64, cross-checked entry-by-entry against 依字母排序 p65–115). **6,012 entries, 1,002 per level.** The 附錄 (numbers, days, months, seasons, countries, continents, oceans, religions, parts of speech) is reproduced at the end.

## How to use this file

- **Line format**: `headword (part of speech) [level]`, one entry per line, grouped by level and in the official order (alphabetical within a level). Find a word's level with grep, e.g. `grep -i "^- abandon " references/vocabulary.md`, or run `scripts/vocab_level.py` on a whole passage.
- **Scope rule (考試說明)**: 「詞彙範圍以高中英文常用 4,500 字詞為主，可參考大考中心高中英文參考詞彙表第一至第五級，為保留真實語言的使用，偶爾會有第六級（含）以上詞彙。」 So levels 1–5 are the core; level 6 and off-list words appear only occasionally, usually in passages rather than as tested answers.
- **Official notation** (編輯原則):
  - `a/b`: variant spellings, informal forms, synonyms or gender pairs listed as one entry (e.g. `bicycle/bike`, `hero/heroine`). The word after the slash is not listed again.
  - `word(ment) v./(n.)`: the verb also forms a -ment noun with the same meaning (e.g. `agree(ment)`).
  - `word(s)`: the noun is usually plural (e.g. `chopstick(s)`).
  - Parts of speech: n. 名詞, v. 動詞, adj. 形容詞, adv. 副詞, prep. 介系詞, conj. 連接詞, pron. 代名詞, art. 冠詞, aux. 助動詞. Most frequent part of speech first.
- **Regular derivatives are deliberately not listed**: adverbs in -ly (unless the meaning differs, e.g. roughly, scarcely), nouns in -ness, negative adjectives with in-/im-/ir-/il-/un-, most non- words (except nonsense, nonprofit), adjectives in -less, verbs with re-, and -ing/-ed adjectives (unless the meaning differs, e.g. promising, learned). Treat such a derivative as sharing its base word's level. Most compounds, proper nouns and interjections are also excluded.
- Spelling follows American English.
