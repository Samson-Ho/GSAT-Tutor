# 英文 — Trends (考試說明 → 參考試卷 → 110試辦 → 111–115學測)

GSAT-Tutor's analysis of how the 考試說明 has actually been applied. These are observed patterns, not official policy: never tell a student a topic "will" appear.

## 1. Structure: stable since 111

Takeaways:
- **Default 混合題 since 112**: 47–48 填充 (one word from the passage each, with word-form change, 2+2) → 49 多選 (4) → 50 簡答 (a phrase from the passage matching a given definition, 2).
- **篇章結構 now has 5 options for 4 blanks.** Expect one distractor that fits the topic but breaks the logic.
- Total 100: 62 / 10 / 28.

| 參考 | tropical glaciers | round plane windows | stunt people | roller-coaster design | flip-flops | bar pilots (ships) | zebrafish | refugee Olympians |

Patterns:
- **Genre**: mostly expository and informational, from news, magazines and popular-science websites (the 試辦 and 參考試卷 解析 cite sources such as BBC, Smithsonian, Britannica, Reader's Digest, Forbes). Narrative appears inside history or biography passages. Each year mixes science and health, history and invention, culture and society, and nature and animals.
- **Origin stories and "who really invented X"** recur (Monopoly, hard hat, Velcro, traffic light, hula hoop, spiral staircase, map orientation).
- **Myth-busting or "more to the story" framing** (Russia and alcohol, "is water always best?", horror films, traffic-light credit). Inference items often hinge on the author's corrective stance.
- **Taiwan and Asia connections** appear but are not dominant (Obon, capsule hotels, dim sum, "Wonder Village" (Taiwanese indigenous), wind phone).
- **Multimodal**: picture-matching items (111-35 Maasai warrior, 113-35 Velcro, 114-36 traffic signal design, 115-38 *Endurance* route map), maps (112-44 Zanzibar), time-zone diagrams (試辦).
- **混合題 genres**: parallel profiles (refugee athletes), **forums and chatrooms with many short posts** (112, 114), compare-two-things science (113), and **store or listing directories** (115). Expect real-world, multi-voice formats.

## 3. 詞彙題 keys: level profile (111–115, 50 items)

| Level | Count | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | 1 | stand (the test of time) |
| L2 | 5 | deliver, pose (n.), due, promise, produce |
| L3 | 13 | assistant, puppet, tag, visible, sticky, bare, slender, credit, appeal, vacant, tight, passion, elbow |
| L4 | 21 | potentially, agreeable, eventual, ancestor, sincerity, recall, counter, choke, emerge, humidity, container, offensive, draft, enormous, halt, grace, amateur, vacancy, initially, dread, grave |
| L5 | 10 | quest, blush, cripple, supposedly, compulsory, randomly, blur, verbally, consumption, assault |

Takeaways:
- **Target mainly levels 3–5, with level 4 as the core.** No level-6 keys.
- Low-level keys test a **secondary sense or a collocation** (stand the test of time; the bus is due; adopt a pose). These are worth explicit teaching.
- **Adverbs in -ly** (potentially, supposedly, randomly, verbally, initially) and **derivatives** (agreeable, vacancy, consumption) appear every year: word formation (構詞) matters.
- Distractors share the key's part of speech and form, and often its level.

Takeaways: the two sentences are **thematically linked**, usually on a current social topic. Words are mostly 詞彙表 levels 1–4, and each sentence carries **one key grammar point** (passive, perfect, relative clause, participle, comparison, not…but, as long as). Since 114 the official notes name the grammar points explicitly.

## 6. 英文作文 (20分)

| Year | Prompt | Paragraph 1 | Paragraph 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 110試辦 | 2 pictures: an empty reservoir (water shortage) | describe the pictures (人、事、物、發生的事) | possible causes + concrete measures for the future |
| 111 | 2 pictures of different parks | features of park A and park B | your ideal park and why |
| 112 | 4 emoji | why people like emoji + function of 1–2 examples | a personal or friend's misunderstanding caused by emoji + how to resolve it |
| 113 | 3 captioned pictures of teen problems (First Heartbreak / Caring What Others Think / Mood Swings) | which problem a robot helper should solve, and why | the robot's traits and abilities + how you'd work together |
| 114 | 2 contrasting pictures of a typhoon day off | two different scenes that may happen | your view and experience of typhoon days off |
| 115 | pictures on pets' changing role | describe the phenomenon in the pictures | causes + possible effects, from experience or observation |
| 參考 | = 111 parks | | |
| 考試說明 | 看圖 (anniversary-sale chaos; tourists at a site), 信函 (e-mail welcoming an exchange student), 主題 (housework division) | | |

Takeaways:
- **Always two paragraphs**, with the content of each specified. The usual pattern is **describe (picture/phenomenon) → explain or evaluate (reasons, opinion, experience, solution)**.
- **Every 111–115 prompt is picture-based** (看圖寫作). 信函 and 主題 writing are in the 考試說明 but haven't appeared since 111. Practise them as variants.
- Topics come from teen daily life and social trends: parks, emoji, teen problems, typhoon days, pets.
- Missing either paragraph's required content caps **內容** and often **組織** (「未按提示寫作」 can mean 0 for 組織).

| Section | Spec |
|---|---|
| 詞彙 1–10 | Keys: two L3, five L4, three L5 (one key in a secondary sense or collocation; one -ly adverb); distractors of the same part of speech and level |
| 綜合 11–20 | 2 passages (~200–250 words), mixing 3 grammar blanks, 3 transition or discourse blanks and 4 lexical blanks |
| 文意選填 21–30 | 1 passage (~300 words), 10 options (A)–(J): about 3 nouns, 3 verbs (in a set form), 2–3 adjectives, 1–2 phrases or adverbs |
| 篇章 31–34 | 1 passage (~300 words), 4 blanks, 5 options (1 distractor) |
| 閱讀 35–46 | 3 passages (300–400 words): science or health, history or invention, culture or society; one item with a picture or map; mix of item types per §4 |
| 混合 47–50 | Multi-voice or parallel text (forum, profiles, listings); 47–48 填充 with word-form change, 49 多選 (6–10 options), 50 簡答 phrase-for-definition |
| 中譯英 | 2 linked sentences on one social topic, each with one key structure |
| 作文 | Picture-based, 2 paragraphs (describe → explain or evaluate), ≥120 words |

## 8. Study priorities

1. **Levels 3–5 vocabulary with collocations and word families** (`vocabulary.md`). Learn secondary senses of common words.
2. **Discourse signals** for 綜合 and 篇章結構: transitions, pronoun reference, topic sentences, the "one extra option" trap.
3. **Reading item types**: reference, vocabulary in context, inference vs detail, purpose and organization, picture matching.
4. **混合題 precision**: copy the exact word or phrase from the passage, then change its form correctly (participle, gerund, noun form). A form or spelling error loses 1 of 2 points.
5. **中譯英 accuracy**: one structure per sentence, articles and number, tense, spelling, capitals and punctuation. Each error costs 0.5.
6. **Two-paragraph essay discipline**: cover every prompt requirement, give specific details, use transitions, vary sentences, write ≥120 words.
