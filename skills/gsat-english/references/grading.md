# 英文 — Grading 作文／中譯英／混合題, Writing 評分原則, Diagnosing Weaknesses

## 1. 英文作文 (20分): 四項分項式評分

### 1.1 Official rubric (考試說明 p32; 111–115 評分原則 表一, verbatim)

**英文作文分項式評分標準**: four 項目 (內容、組織、文法句構、字彙拼字), each **0–5分**, total **20分**.

| 項目 | 優 (5–4分) | 可 (3分) | 差 (2–1分) | 劣 (0分) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **內容** | 主題（句）清楚切題，並有具體、完整的相關細節支持。 | 主題不夠清楚或突顯，部分相關敘述發展不全。 | 主題不明，大部分相關敘述發展不全或與主題無關。 | 文不對題或沒寫（凡文不對題或沒寫者，其他各項均以零分計算）。 |
| **組織** | 重點分明，有開頭、發展、結尾，前後連貫，轉承語使用得當。 | 重點安排不妥，前後發展比例與轉承語使用欠妥。 | 重點不明、前後不連貫。 | 全文毫無組織或未按提示寫作。 |
| **文法、句構** | 全文幾無文法、格式、標點錯誤，文句結構富變化。 | 文法、格式、標點錯誤少，且未影響文意之表達。 | 文法、格式、標點錯誤多，且明顯影響文意之表達。 | 全文文法錯誤嚴重，導致文意不明。 |
| **字彙、拼字** | 用字精確、得宜，且幾無拼字、大小寫錯誤。 | 字詞單調、重複，用字偶有不當，少許拼字、大小寫錯誤，但不影響文意之表達。 | 用字、拼字、大小寫錯誤多，明顯影響文意之表達。 | 只寫出或抄襲與題意有關的零碎字詞。 |

### 1.2 How the score is formed (111–115 評分原則, verbatim)

> 英文作文依考生在內容、組織、文法句構、字彙拼字之表現評分，各項得分加總後給予一個整體分數（holistic score），再依總分 1 至 20 分，分為下述五等級：特優（19-20 分）、優（15-18 分）、可（10-14 分）、差（5-9 分）、劣（0-4 分）。閱卷委員在仔細評閱考生的作答內容後，再依其內容是否切題、組織是否具連貫性、句子結構與文法、用字是否適切表達文意，及拼字與標點符號使用是否正確等要項，進行評分；……字數明顯不足者，扣總分 1 分；未分段者，亦扣總分 1 分。

- The 115 wording drops the five-band sentence but keeps the four-part sum and the deductions.
- **Deductions**: 字數明顯不足 (clearly under 120 words) −1; 未分段 (the prompt asks for two paragraphs) −1.
- **Zero rule**: 文不對題或沒寫 → 內容 0 **and every other part 0**.
- 評分重點 (every year's commentary): 「內容須符合題目要求，提供充分、具體之細節，句構語法及用字適切，以及拼字與標點符號使用得當；內容充實以及語言能力表現佳應是考生得分之重要元素」.

### 1.3 Grading procedure (essay)

1. **Prompt compliance first**: two paragraphs? Does each paragraph do what the prompt assigns (e.g. 112: P1 why people like emoji + 1–2 example functions; P2 a misunderstanding + a solution)? List the requirements and tick them.
2. **內容 (0–5)**: clear topic sentences on task, with specific, complete supporting details for every required point. A missing required point caps 內容 at 3; several missing, 2–1.
3. **組織 (0–5)**: clear focus per paragraph, an opening, development and a closing, logical order, appropriate transitions. Not following the prompt's paragraph plan → 3 or lower; 「未按提示寫作」 can mean 0.
4. **文法句構 (0–5)**: count and weigh errors (tense, agreement, articles, fragments and run-ons, word order, punctuation). 5–4 needs almost no errors **and** sentence variety (complex sentences, participles, relative clauses, inversion used correctly). Frequent errors that obscure meaning → 2–1.
5. **字彙拼字 (0–5)**: precise, appropriate word choice and collocations; spelling and capitals. Repetitive or plain vocabulary → 3 even if error-free.
6. **Sum, then deductions**: total = 四項加總; −1 if clearly under 120 words; −1 if not paragraphed. Report the band (特優／優／可／差／劣).
7. **Calibrate**: most solid 高中 essays score 10–15. 19–20 needs fully developed content *and* near-flawless, varied language. When torn, give the lower score and say what would raise it.

## 2. 中譯英 (8分 = 2 × 4)

### 2.1 Official rules (111–115 評分原則, verbatim)
> 1. 本大題總分 8 分，每小題滿分 4 分。
> 2. 每個錯誤扣 0.5 分，各部分獨立，扣完為止。
> 3. 相同之拼字錯誤或文法錯誤，只扣一次。
> 4. 句首未大寫或標點符號不妥，各扣 0.5 分，只扣一次。

Annual commentary: 「考生須將兩個中文句子譯成正確、通順且達意的兩個英文句子……原則上是每個錯誤扣 0.5 分，相同的錯誤只扣一次」; 「考生必須句法使用正確，且用字、拼字皆正確才能得到滿分」; 「所評量的字詞大致都以詞彙表一至四級詞彙為主」.

### 2.2 Grading procedure
1. Identify the **target words** and the **key structure** (the official commentary names them; see `question-index.md` and `trends.md` §5).
2. Read the student's sentence as segments ("各部分獨立"). For each segment, accept any correct, natural rendering (the official 參考答案 lists alternatives with stacked options), not only the reference wording.
3. Deduct **0.5 per error**: wrong or missing word, wrong form, tense, agreement, article, preposition, word order, spelling. Repeated identical errors count once. A missing capital at the start or bad punctuation costs 0.5 once.
4. A segment omitted or mistranslated so that meaning is lost: deduct for each missing element. Score floor 0 per sentence.
5. Report: 「第1題 3／4（扣：……0.5；……0.5）」, then the corrected sentence and one or two alternative full-mark versions.

## 3. 混合題 非選 (填充／簡答, 2分 each)

Official rule (112–115, verbatim):
> （一）完全正確（包括選字及字形變化），得 2 分。
> （二）字形錯誤或者拼字錯誤，得 1 分。
> （三）空白、答案錯誤或與答案無關者，得 0 分。

110試辦 wording of the 1-point tier: 「答案不完整（未做字形變化）或拼字、體例（大小寫、定冠詞等）錯誤，得 1 分」.

Practical rules:
- **填充**: the word must come **from the passage** (when the item says so) and take the form the blank needs (e.g. participate → participating; confine → confined; innovate → innovation). Right word, wrong form → 1. Wrong word → 0.
- **簡答**: copy the exact word or phrase from the passage that matches the definition (asylum; does the trick; fostering empathy; one of a kind). Official keys accept capitalized or sentence forms (「asylum/Asylum.」「fostering empathy / Fostering empathy.」). Extra words that change the answer → usually 0. A misspelling → 1.
- Accept every listed alternative (e.g. 47 entertaining/educating; 50 a predator/a fox).
- **多選** in the 混合題 follows the choice formula (`scope.md` §4).

## 4. Writing rubrics for generated items

- **Essay**: always state the four-part rubric (§1.1). Add a **prompt-specific checklist**: what P1 and P2 must contain, plus 2–3 language features a 優 essay would show. Then give a sample outline. Give a model essay only on request, labelled 「GSAT-Tutor 範文（非官方）」, 150–200 words, at a level a strong 高中 student could reach.
- **中譯英**: give 參考答案 with alternatives in brackets (e.g. "History has {proven/proved} time and again…"), list the target words and the key structure, and restate the four official rules.
- **混合題**: give the key with accepted alternatives and the 2/1/0 rule.
- Label every rubric 「GSAT-Tutor 評分原則（依大考中心格式擬定）」.

## 5. Weakness diagnosis

| Area | Signal | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 詞彙 (T1) | Misses on L3–L5 keys and collocations | Level-targeted lists from `vocabulary.md`; collocation notebooks; word families (-ly, -ion, -able) |
| 語法 in context (T2) | Tense, participle or voice blanks in 綜合測驗 | Grammar-in-passage drills: decide subject–verb relation, time and voice first |
| 篇章 (T3, T4) | 文意選填 part-of-speech slips; 篇章結構 distractor chosen | Pre-label blanks by part of speech; for 篇章, check pronoun and transition links both before and after the blank |
| 閱讀 (T5) | Pattern by item type (reference, inference, vocab in context, picture) | Item-type drills; justify each answer with a line number |
| 混合 (T6) | Right word, wrong form; extra words in 簡答 | Practise "find, copy, transform" with word-form tables |
| 中譯英 (T7) | Articles and number, tense, missing structure, spelling | Error log by type; one structure per sentence; final 30-second check (capital, period, s/es, the/a) |
| 作文 (T8) | Prompt points missed; thin details; repetitive syntax; transitions | Two-paragraph template with topic sentence → 2–3 specific details → wrap-up; sentence-combining practice |
