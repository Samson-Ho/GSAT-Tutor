## 111學測
- **詞彙 1–10**: delivered, assistant, puppet, potentially, tagging, agreeable, visible, eventual, quest, blushes.
- **綜合**: 11–15 book towns (initiated / have been springing / end / releases / keep the printed word alive); 16–20 round airplane windows.
- **文意選填** 21–30: stunt people (risky, necessity, professional, tried out, cost, called for, career, witnessed, possible, sensation).
- **篇章** 31–34: Obon festival (4 options).
- **閱讀**: 35–38 Maasai (35 picture of a warrior; 38 why quote Blixen); 39–42 hard hats (39 NOT discussed; 40 order; 42 words referring to the hard hat); 43–46 zebrafish (45 "replicated"; 46 opinion vs fact).
- **混合** 47–49: refugee Olympians Yusra Mardini and Popole Misenga. 47 (A) participating/swimming, (B) abuse/cruelty (填空 4); 48 asylum (簡答 2); 49 CD (多選 4).
- **中譯英**: 飼養寵物並非一項短暫的人生體驗，而是一個對動物的終生承諾。／在享受寵物所帶來的歡樂時，我們不該忽略要善盡照顧他們的責任。
- **作文**: two parks (A, B). P1 features of each; P2 your ideal park and reasons.

## 112學測
- **詞彙** keys: sticky, pose, ancestors, bare, sincerity, recall, counter, crippled, choked, supposedly (9 choked = 考試說明 例3).
- **綜合**: 11–15 crown shyness; 16–20 gravity and Mars missions.
- **文意選填**: is plain water always best (hydration).
- **篇章**: synesthesia.
- **閱讀**: 35–38 hula hoop (35 what question the passage answers; 37 NOT mentioned; 38 materials); 39–42 Asian elephants in Assam tea gardens (42 "it"); 43–46 Zanzibar (44 map; 45 inference; 46 words referring to Zanzibar).
- **混合** 47–50: GreenBurger chatroom (8 posts). 47–48 taste/health; 49 DF (choice only under certain circumstances); 50 does the trick.
- **中譯英**: 歷史一再證明，戰爭會造成極為可怕的災難。／避免衝突、確保世界和平應該是所有人類追求的目標。
- **作文**: emoji. P1 why people like emoji + functions of 1–2 of the given emoji; P2 a misunderstanding or trouble from personal or friends' experience + how to resolve it.

## 113學測
- **詞彙** keys: slender, emerging, due, humidity, stand, compulsory, credit, promises, appeal, randomly.
- **綜合**: 11–15 mystery shoppers; 16–20 how people woke up before alarm clocks.
- **文意選填**: the "wind phone" in Otsuchi, Japan.
- **篇章**: exercising when injured (Dr. Weinstein).
- **閱讀**: 35–38 Velcro (35 picture of the invention; 36 "a lightbulb moment"; 38 NOT mentioned); 39–42 Thomas Moran (40 "the blackened heart"; 42 inference); 43–46 Iceland's "100% Fish" and fish-skin grafts (44 "this pioneering procedure"; 46 sentence insertion).
- **混合** 47–50: jackdaws and turtle ants. 47 group/collective; 48 adapting; 49 CF; 50 a predator / a fox.
- **中譯英**: 每逢選舉季節，總會看到政治人物造訪各地著名廟宇。／除了祈求好的選舉結果，他們也希望展現對在地文化與習俗的尊重。
- **作文**: a robot helper for one of three teen problems (First Heartbreak / Caring What Others Think / Mood Swings). P1 which problem and why; P2 the robot's traits and abilities and how you'd cooperate.

- **作文**: typhoon days off. P1 two contrasting scenes that may actually happen (from the pictures); P2 your view and experience.

## 115學測
- **詞彙** keys: tight, amateur, vacancy, initially, consumption, dreads, passion, assaulted, elbow, grave.
- **綜合**: 11–15 white rhinos' dung piles as "news hubs"; 16–20 dim sum and the Silk Road.
- **文意選填**: daytime napping.
- **篇章** (first 4-blank / 5-option set): smiling across cultures (distractor among (A)–(E)).
- **閱讀**: 35–38 Shackleton's *Endurance* (36 idiom closest to "exacerbated the situation"; 38 route map); 39–42 spiral staircases (40 "them"; 41 time sequence of materials; 42 inference); 43–46 why north is at the top of maps (44 "this"; 45 "a blip").
- **混合** 47–50: "Wonder Village" specialty shops (A–F). 47 innovation; 48 blended; 49 ADE (antique lamp, recycled sunglasses, aboriginal necklace → stores); 50 one of a kind.
- **中譯英**: 現在越來越多高中英文老師已經增加在課堂上使用英文的百分比。／他們將學生依英語能力分成不同組別，進行多樣的聽、說活動。 (present perfect; S+V with optional participial construction)
- **作文**: pets' changing role in Taiwan. P1 describe the phenomenon in the pictures; P2 causes and possible effects, from experience or observation.
