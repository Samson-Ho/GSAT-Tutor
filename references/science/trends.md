# 自然 — Trends (參考試卷／109–110試辦 → 111–115學測)

GSAT-Tutor's analysis of how the 考試說明 scope has actually been tested. Use it to weight topics, choose item styles, build mock papers and prioritize study plans. These are observed patterns, not official policy: never tell a student a topic "will" appear.

## 1. Paper structure

| | 參考試卷 | 109試辦 | 110試辦 | 111 | 112 | 113 | 114 | 115 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 第壹部分 | 1–36 (72) | 1–36 | 1–36 | 1–36 | 1–36 | 1–36 | 1–36 | 1–36 |
| 第貳部分 題組 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 |
| 最後題號 | 56 | 56 | 60 | 60 | 60 | 56 | 57 | 56 |
| 非選題數／配分 | 8／32 | 8／32 | 8／24 | 8／24 | 8／24 | 8／32 | 9／32 | 8／32 |

- 110 分鐘, 128 分 total (72 + 56). Every 選擇題 is worth 2 分, including those in Part 2.
- **Part 1 = four blocks of 9 questions, one per discipline.** The block order rotates every year:

| Year | 1–9 | 10–18 | 19–27 | 28–36 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 參考試卷 | 地 | 化 | 物 | 生 |
| 111 | 物 | 化 | 生 | 地 |
| 112 | 地 | 生 | 化 | 物 |
| 113 | 生 | 化 | 物 | 地 |
| 114 | 生 | 地 | 物 | 化 |
| 115 | 地 | 物 | 化 | 生 |

- **Part 2 = 6 題組**, each 3–6 items, mixing 選擇 (2 分 each) and 非選 (2 or 4 分). Since 113, every 非選 is usually 4 分 (114 has two 2-分 items).
- **非選 are distributed evenly: 2 per discipline** (114: 化學 3). Each 題組 typically carries 1–2 非選.
- The four disciplines carry roughly equal weight (參考試卷: 原則上比重大致相當).

## 2. Item style

- **多選 always state 「應選 n 項」.** In 111–115, 多2 is the most common and 多3 is frequent. Composite forms show up about once a year: 「甲、乙、丙三欄各選1項」 (參考-6), 「A至C選1項、D至F選1項」 (參考-50, 112-6 also has six options for two choices).
- Options are (A)–(E), sometimes (F). Negative stems (「何者錯誤」「最不可能」「不正確」「不可行」) appear 3–5 times per paper.
- **Single-sentence knowledge items are a minority.** Most Part 1 items carry a short context: a news event, an experiment, a figure or a data table. Several Part 1 items come in 2-question mini-groups (e.g. 111-24~25, 113-12~13, 114-28~29).
- **Graph and table reading is everywhere.** Expect 10–15 items per paper whose answer hinges on reading a figure, table or chart (phase diagrams, solubility curves, weather maps, seismograms, v–t / a–x graphs, gene-expression bar charts, isotope ratio cores).
- **Light computation** with data given in the stem: moles and mass ratios, molarity, energy and power, wave speed, $E=h\nu$, $E=mc^2$, Kepler ratios, travel times. Numbers are designed for hand calculation. Order-of-magnitude answers (數量級) are common (112-30, 114-20~22, 115-52).

Patterns:
- **Long 文本** (250–600 字) with figures or tables; the knowledge needed is 必修 level, but you must extract the information from the passage.
- **About half the 題組 are cross-disciplinary** (跨科), often pairing 物＋地, 化＋生, 物＋生 or 化＋地.
- **Hooks**: recent **Nobel Prizes** (most years have 1–2 題組 or items tied to the previous one or two years' prizes), **current events** (COVID and mRNA vaccines, Mars missions, a real 2024 typhoon (康芮), Bitcoin's energy use, AI), **Taiwan context** (颱風, 地震預警, 潮汐, 虱目魚養殖, 臺灣地質), **technology and sustainability** (碳捕捉／CO₂ 還原, 光觸媒, 潮汐發電, 量子點, EUV 晶片製程).
- The final 題組 is often 地科 or 物理 with a quantitative or graphing 非選.

## 5. Topic frequency by discipline (111–115)

**地球科學**: the most stable themes.
- **颱風**: every year (structure, pressure and wind change as it passes, track steered by 太平洋高壓, 西北颱, Taiwan-crossing effects such as 焚風 and 西南氣流, 藤原效應).
- **天文**: every year (星等, 電磁波段 and 大氣窗, star colour and temperature, solar-system bodies, celestial sphere, eclipses, 光行時間).
- Every year or nearly: **地震／板塊／臺灣地質** (P/S waves and 地震預警, fault types, convergent boundaries), **大氣水氣** (飽和水氣壓, 相對溼度, 抬升凝結, 潛熱), **潮汐** (3 of 5), **海洋** (鹽度, 洋流, 黑潮, 聖嬰), **氣候變遷／古氣候** (time scales, 冰期, proxies), **地質年代／放射性定年** (碳14 vs 鈾鉛 suitability; half-life counting).

**物理**:
- **基本交互作用**: every year (which force dominates; ranges; strong force inside nuclei; weak decay of neutrons).
- **電磁感應 (冷次定律)**: nearly every year, often as a Part 2 graphing or reasoning 非選.
- **都卜勒效應**: nearly every year (blood flow, spacecraft, exoplanets).
- **Energy conversion and efficiency** with real-world numbers: every year.
- Frequent: **波動與光** (干涉, 繞射, 折射, 雙狹縫 for light and electrons), **光子／能階／光電效應**, **核能／質能**, **運動學與牛頓定律** (graphs, net force direction), **SI 單位與因次**, **科學史**.

**化學**:
- Every year: **化學計量** (mole calculations from data), **氧化還原** (identify, oxidant choice, balance equations), **化學鍵／路易斯結構** (lone pairs, double bonds).
- Very frequent: **酸鹼與指示劑** (pH ranges, ordering [H⁺]), **相圖** (111, 112, 115; plus 109/110 試辦), **溶解度** (curves, recrystallization, gas solubility), **分離純化** (層析, 萃取, 蒸餾, 過濾, 沉澱), **週期表趨勢**.
- Recurring contexts: environmental chemistry (black carbon, Cr(VI), smog, CO₂ reduction, photocatalysis), biomolecules and polymers, combustion heats.

**生物**:
- Every year: **分類與親緣樹** (reading or redrawing cladograms; homologous vs analogous structures; viruses and classification), **分子遺傳** (DNA/RNA structure, transcription and translation location, mRNA vaccines, gene expression data).
- Very frequent: **細胞分裂** (DNA content through the cycle, mitosis vs meiosis, chromosome counts), **孟德爾遺傳與譜系** (ABO, sex-linked, autosomal-dominant pedigrees, deviations from expected ratios), **演化與天擇／人擇**, **光合作用與呼吸作用**, **實驗觀察** (撕取法, 染色, DNA 萃取), **細胞學說／遺傳學史**.

## 6. Cognitive profile

- Official tags concentrate on 2a/2c (data interpretation), 3b (explain phenomena with laws or models), and 4b/4d (cause–effect and scientific analysis). 非選 add 5a/5b.
- 探究與實作 is tested directly: controlled variables, choosing comparison groups, identifying 過度推論, what evidence would test a claim, table titles and units, plotting conventions.
- Science-history items test **who did what and why it mattered**: photoelectric effect (Einstein; not first discovered by him), atomic models (Thomson → Rutherford → Bohr), electromagnetism (Oersted, Faraday, Maxwell, Hertz), cell theory, genetics history.

## 7. What changed from 參考試卷／試辦 to 111–115

- 非選 were 24 分 in 110試辦 and 111–112, then returned to 32 分 from 113 (as in 參考試卷 and 109試辦).
- Part 1 kept the four-block structure but rotates the order.
- Contexts moved even more toward recent research and Nobel topics, and cross-disciplinary 題組 became standard.

## 8. Mock-paper blueprint (default for a full 自然 模擬卷)

**第壹部分 (36 題, 72 分)**: four blocks of 9 (rotate the order). In each block, 4–5 單選 and 4–5 多選 (mostly 應選2項, some 應選3項), 1–2 negative stems, at least 3 items with figures or tables, and one 2-question mini-group.

Per-block topic menu (choose about 9, varying across mocks):
- **物**: 基本交互作用; 電磁感應; 都卜勒; 波動／光; 光子與能階; 核能與質能; 運動與牛頓定律（graph）; 能量轉換與效率; 萬有引力與衛星; 單位因次; 科學史.
- **化**: 化學計量; 氧化還原; 路易斯結構; 酸鹼指示劑; 相圖; 溶解度; 分離純化; 週期表; 反應熱; 生物分子／界面活性劑; 環境化學.
- **生**: 細胞與分裂; 光合／呼吸; 孟德爾與譜系; 分子遺傳; 演化與天擇; 分類與親緣樹; 病毒; 基因轉殖; 實驗觀察; 科學史.
- **地**: 颱風; 天文; 地震與板塊; 大氣水氣; 潮汐; 海洋與聖嬰; 氣候變遷; 地質年代與定年; 臺灣地質; 永續與能源.

**第貳部分 (6 題組, 56 分)**: 20–24 items in total; 8 非選 (2 per discipline, 4 分 each = 32 分), the rest 選擇 at 2 分. Include at least 3 cross-disciplinary 題組, at least one with a graphing 非選, at least one with an equation-writing or calculation 非選, and at least one 探究 (variables) item. Use one recent Nobel or current-event hook and one Taiwan-specific context.
